LSF Membership

To get started in the LSF, go ahead and download one of the task sheets for either the Soaring Accomplishments Program (SAP) or Electric Soaring Accomplishments Program (eSAP) and start flying!

Upon completion of the tasks for Level 1, scan your completed Level 1 form and return it to the LSF Board and you will be given an LSF member number. This member number will be the same for both the SAP or eSAP and is good for life.

There are no membership dues with LSF. The LSF operates on the honor system and your cooperation will assure prompt attention to your voucher submissions.
